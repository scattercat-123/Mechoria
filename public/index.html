01 About the event

Arena energy.
Builder minds.

Mechoria is Bengaluru's student-run BattleBots-style tournament. We bring together young builders, coders, tinkerers and strategists for an arena where engineering becomes a spectator sport.

Teams design, build and battle their own robots — learning mechanical design, electronics, code, and the joy of making something real along the way.
