Who We Are

Bengaluru's first true BattleBots-style tournament, built by students

We're a team of 8th-semester–stage students working to bring together the most passionate builders, coders, tinkerers, and tech enthusiasts from across the city and create a high-energy arena where engineering meets strategy. Mechoria challenges students in robotics, mechanics, electronics, tactics, and real-world problem-solving — it's a platform to showcase innovation, teamwork, and the future of engineering talent in Bengaluru.

How It Works

Three stages, from first sketch to final battle

Teams design and prep online, ship their parts, then bring it all together for two days of live qualifiers, battles, and finals.

Build-Event

2–4 weeks, online. Workshops, challenges, and mentorship while teams design their bot and put together their CAD model and BOM.

Pre-Event

1 week. Parts ship out, teams build their bot, and run their own tests before arriving at the venue.

Main Event

2 days, offline. Qualifiers, battles, finals, and awards inside the Mechoria Arena.

Weight Categories

Three classes. One arena. Pick your fight.

Every bot competes in one of three weight classes, each with its own build limit and grant support. Ship a proper 3D model and bill of materials for your bot and you qualify for a cash grant toward your build.

No grant, no problem — teams can also skip the grant and build with their own parts. Note: bots that exceed their class weight limit are disqualified.

How the grant works: to receive a grant, your team needs to properly ship the project — that means a 3D model and a bill of materials (BOM).

This isn't just paperwork for its own sake: it means other teams (and future ones) can actually see how your bot was built, and maybe build on it themselves. You're always welcome to skip the 3D model and BOM and enter without a grant instead.
